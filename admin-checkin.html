Card-on-file email

rarely needs changing — tap to open
The save-a-card link goes out automatically with the guest's signed licence. This is the email "Resend card link" sends if they haven't done it. Placeholders: [FIRST_NAME], [NAME], [CHECKIN], [CHECKOUT], [AMOUNT] (the charge cap), [LINK]. The [LINK] is added automatically at the end if you leave it out.
