CHECK-IN INSTRUCTIONS

Howqua Hideaway  |  484 Howqua River Road, Howqua VIC 3723
Save these instructions before you leave — mobile data drops on Howqua River Rd.

Directions

  1. Google Maps: View Route (approx. 9 mins of dirt road).
  2. From Mansfield-Woods Point Rd, cross the Howqua River bridge and turn left at the letterboxes onto Howqua River Rd.
  3. Pass the "Lauriston Girls' School" (Private Property sign on the right).
  4. The cabin driveway is 350m past the school on the right (watch closely!). Look for our sign on the unlocked front gate.

Access

  • Drive to the left side of the cabin near the handrail.
  • The lockbox hangs on the right as you reach the top of the handrail.
  • Slide the cover down, enter the code from your booking confirmation, and pull the toggle down to release the front door key.
  • (Note: The rear door can only be locked/unlocked from the outside.)

Settling In

  • Arriving in the dark? Light switches are just inside to the right.
  • Check the welcome folder on the kitchen island for Wi-Fi, contact numbers, and house info.
